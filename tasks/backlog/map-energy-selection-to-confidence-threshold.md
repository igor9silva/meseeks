The default energy selector should communicate diminishing returns as a minimum confidence threshold, not just as a budget number.

Rough mapping from the original capture:

- `$0.20` ~= any answer above `80%` confidence is acceptable
- `$1` ~= `90%`
- `$10` ~= `99%`
- `$1000` ~= `99.99%`

This is a small UX task: help the user understand that more energy buys confidence and verification, not magic.
