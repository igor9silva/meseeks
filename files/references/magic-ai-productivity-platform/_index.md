# Magic AI Productivity Platform

## Reference

Magic / Magicrew is an open-source AI agent platform combining a generalist assistant, workflow engine, collaboration surfaces, sandboxing, budget controls, approval gates, and deliverable generation.

Meseeks relevance:

- personal assistant plus expert-agent framing
- agent workflow engine as a product surface
- human approval for high-risk actions
- budget and cost-control framing
- sandbox/security posture
- deliverables beyond plain chat output

Source:

- https://github.com/dtyq/magic
