Vercel for Platforms is a Vercel product for building and running customer projects on behalf of users.

Useful shape:

- Multi-tenant mode: one codebase serves many customers, with wildcard domains, custom domains, edge routing, SSL, and one deployment applying to all tenants.
- Multi-project mode: one Vercel project per customer, with isolated builds, functions, environment variables, settings, domains, and framework support.
- Platform Elements: UI/library support for building platform workflows.
